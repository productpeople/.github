# Product People 👋

We're a Product Management Consultancy. We help companies discover & deliver great products faster.

Our Interim/Fractional Product Managers, Owners, and Leaders quickly fill gaps, scale your team, or lead key initiatives during transitions. We onboard swiftly, align teams, and deliver results.

## What you'll find here

This is our open-source home. As product managers who build, we publish the 
tools, dashboards, and utilities we actually use in our work — free for the 
PM community to use, fork, and improve.

Everything here is built by PMs, for PMs. No engineering team required.

## Who we are

Product People is a team of product managers offering consulting and 
product management as a service. We believe PMs should be builders — and 
we're putting that into practice.
